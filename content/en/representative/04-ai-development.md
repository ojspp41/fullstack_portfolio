Classify test failures, browser differences, code-review findings, and QA defects by cause, then use them as input for the next task. Thirty-six Skills define execution conditions, inputs, and verification criteria for repeatable work.

Automated the workflow for five usage guides and 93 actual screen images. Service usage-video production went from approximately three days to four hours. Code collects, normalizes, aggregates, and cross-validates operational data; AI writes explanations of major changes and unusual metrics.

Task durations are distinct from infrastructure benchmarks or a universal productivity claim. Actual internal screens and data are not published here.
